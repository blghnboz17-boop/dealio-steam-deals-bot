DEALIO / HELP

Ready in three steps.

1. Connect your Steam profile

Open Dealio’s /setup command in Discord. Enter your Steam profile link or SteamID64. Your wishlist must be public. Never share your Steam password.

2. Select region and language

Choose your Steam account’s actual store country, then Turkish or English. Prices remain in the currency returned by Steam. No estimated currency conversion is used.

3. Confirm notifications

Read the summary and enable DM alerts. Open /dealio for your control center. Use /wishlist to search games, open details, set a price target or mute a game.

Missing notifications?

Check the alert history in the 🔔 Alerts tab first. Do-not-disturb hours or a daily digest may hold an alert. Check Discord DM privacy settings and use “Retry DM access”. After a successful test, re-enable notifications.

An old panel expired?

Panels are private to your account and valid for a limited time. Open a new /dealio command. Tracking continues after a panel closes.

Stop tracking

Pause notifications in settings or delete your account with /delete-data.
