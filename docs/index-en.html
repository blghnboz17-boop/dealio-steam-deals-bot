Good to know

Are notifications instant?

Checks repeat about 30 minutes after a completed scan. Steam and Discord limits may delay delivery. Instant detection is not guaranteed.

Does it show all-time lows?

No. It shows price changes observed by Dealio over the last 90 days. Earlier prices are unknown.

Can I delete my data?

Use /delete-data in Discord and confirm. Retention and backup details are explained in the privacy policy.
