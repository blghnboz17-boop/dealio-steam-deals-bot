Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Operator and scope

Bilgehan Boz, Çanakkale, Turkey, operates Dealio and is responsible for its processing of your personal data. This policy covers the Discord bot and its public website. Contact: blghnboz17@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

We receive your Discord user ID and command inputs; the public SteamID64 you configure; your country, language, notification consent, delivery preferences and timezone; per-game targets, currency, discounts and mute settings. We store wishlist snapshots including game IDs, names, priority and date added, observed prices, check results and times, rule revisions, delivery status, error codes and Discord message identifiers. Delivery identifiers establish acceptance by Discord, not whether you read a message.

3. Steam and Discord

Validated vanity names may be sent to Valve to resolve a SteamID64. Steam receives the public SteamID64, game IDs, country and language required for price checks. Your Discord ID is not sent to Steam. To show historical low prices in alerts, IsThereAnyDeal receives only Steam game IDs and the Store country, never your Discord or Steam identifiers. Passwords are never requested. We use the Guilds intent, without privileged Message Content, Guild Members or Presence intents; ordinary conversations are not read.

4. Purposes and grounds

Data is used to provide the configuration, price checks, requested notifications, delivery retry, history, deletion and service security you request. Where applicable, processing relies on performance of the requested service, legal obligations, protection of rights and legitimate operational interests subject to your fundamental rights. Consent is obtained where legally required. Data is not sold or used for targeted advertising; no solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made.

5. Hosting and recipients

The production bot and SQLite database run on Microsoft Azure in Sweden Central. The public website uses GitHub Pages. Backups are encrypted before leaving the VM and stored as private GitHub Actions artifacts. The recovery key is stored in a restricted GitHub Actions secret and in the operator’s private recovery copy. Healthchecks.io receives only service, backup and recovery success/failure signals and non-identifying error categories, never user IDs, wishlists or the database. The provider may process network metadata. Discord, Valve, IsThereAnyDeal and Microsoft process necessary service and ordinary network metadata under their respective practices. Data may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect rights. The source repository remains private.

6. Retention

Notification history is displayed for 30 days. Price-change observations are kept for 90 days. Application logs written from 29 September 2026 use seven-day retention; older system logs follow the host’s existing rotation policy. Active queues and deduplication identifiers for continuing offers may remain longer to avoid duplicate delivery. Your current configuration and wishlist snapshot remain while you use the service. Encrypted backup artifacts have a seven-day retention setting; provider deletion can take additional time. Non-identifying recovery evidence is kept for 35 days. The free Healthchecks.io plan keeps the latest 100 signal records per check.

7. Deletion

Confirming /delete-data deletes your configuration, game rules, schedule, snapshots, check state and notification records from the active database. Public game-price observations contain no Discord or Steam user identifiers and can remain for their retention period. Deletion does not remove Discord messages or independent provider records. Existing encrypted backups expire through retention rather than individual editing. Restored data must be reconciled with subsequent deletions before notifications resume.

8. Logs and security

Operational logs contain service state and diagnostic information. Interaction tokens, webhook secrets and authorization values are redacted before application logging. Bot tokens and environment files are excluded from application backups. Access controls, HTTPS, limited gateway permissions and SQLite secure deletion are used. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

9. International transfers and your rights

Discord, Valve, IsThereAnyDeal, Microsoft/GitHub and Healthchecks.io may process data outside Turkey or your country of residence. Transfers for which Dealio is responsible are subject to applicable legal requirements and safeguards; third-party processing is governed by those providers. Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion and information about purposes and recipients, object to qualifying automated results and exercise other rights under applicable data protection law. Contact the operator for requests and identity verification appropriate to the request.

10. Website, children and changes

The website uses no advertising trackers or account login. Hosting providers may process IP and browser information. The service is not directed to children below Discord’s minimum age or any higher applicable minimum age. Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the date and communicated where appropriate.
